📖 Reference Formulas
Ohm's Law $$V = I \cdot R \quad\mid\quad I = \frac{V}{R} \quad\mid\quad R = \frac{V}{I}$$
Power Formulas $$P = V \cdot I \quad\mid\quad P = I^2 \cdot R \quad\mid\quad P = \frac{V^2}{R}$$
Series Circuits $$R_{\text{eq}} = R_1 + R_2 + \dots + R_n$$ $$I_{\text{total}} = I_1 = I_2 = \dots = I_n$$
Parallel Circuits (Reciprocal) $$\frac{1}{R_{\text{eq}}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{R_n}$$ $$R_{\text{eq}} = \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{R_i} \right)^{-1}$$
Voltage Divider (Series) $$V_x = V_s \cdot \left( \frac{R_x}{R_{\text{total}}} \right)$$
